Exit checkout

Checkout matches the items at the exit against the shopper's health plan. Restricted items must be removed before it can finish.

    Scan at the exit

    Scan every item in the physical cart. With nothing scanned, the shopper's phone cart is used.

    Restock route

    Floor bins below their minimum, in walking order. Bring the listed amount from the stockroom.

    Use soon

    Lots that expire within 7 days. Move them to the front of the bin.
